Fixes for the SHOW faders that play scenes, the intensity ones and the speed ones, also from a MIDI controller such as the APC mini.

### Several faders at once

Two faders with scenes of the same exclusive group, moved at the same time, took each other’s place on every movement. The scene of one never got to play and the other one kept starting over: a strobe or an FX got stuck. Now each fader adds its scene to its group and you can raise several at once. On the channels they share, the intensity is the highest one and the rest (colour, position…) comes from the last one you raised; with different priorities, the higher one wins.

### A raised fader doesn’t switch off

A button that played another scene of the group stopped the fader’s scene even though it was raised, and on a controller the fader stayed up with nothing playing. Now, while a fader is raised, only lowering it all the way stops it. It isn’t stopped by other scenes, its own button, “Release groups”, “Stop all”, a playback or the editors.

- Closing or changing the show stops everything, as always.
- If you delete the fader or move it to another scene, its scene keeps playing like any other.
- In the SHOW’s “Playing now” panel, a scene held by a fader shows it with its icon.

### More

- A template on GitHub to tell us whether an interface, a controller or a fixture works with the program.
- The manual up to date in all six languages.
