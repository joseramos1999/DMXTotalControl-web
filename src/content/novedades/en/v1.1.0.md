Complete cuelists, cuelist exchange with QLC+, GDTF-Share from the program and all the small improvements that were still pending.

### GDTF-Share from the program

- Search fixtures on GDTF-Share with your free account: Options › Fixture library › “GDTF-Share…”, or “Search GDTF-Share…” in Add fixtures. The list is saved so you can search offline too.
- “Download and import” converts the fixture as when importing a GDTF, and “Patch it” takes it to Add fixtures.
- It tells you about newer revisions of what you downloaded (once a day at startup, with “Check for updates at startup”) and updates them with “Update to this revision” or “Update all”.
- The password is only saved if you tick “Remember on this computer”, encrypted for your Windows user.
- A mode with several *DMX breaks* gives one more mode for each of them, patched at its own address.

### Cuelists

- **Types**: Normal, Chase (the cues advance on their own at a BPM, with a crossfade and a direction), Submaster (the fader rules), Override (it goes on top of what is playing) and Timecode (each cue at its time, with its own clock, inside a super scene or following MIDI timecode).
- **Times per attribute** (intensity, colour, position and beam) **and per fixture** in “Cue details” › “Times”, with fade **curves** and delay or fade **fans**.
- **Links** (“after cue 5, cue 2, three times”) and **actions** when a cue starts: GO on another cuelist, play or stop a scene, set the BPM…
- **Move in black**: fixtures that are off get into place before their next cue.
- Between cuelists of the same priority, the last change wins on the attributes that are not intensity.
- **Undo and redo** cuelist edits (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y).
- In the playbacks: **flash attack and decay**, a **crossfade fader (A/B)** that makes each GO by hand, **cue speed** for every cuelist and the **live time** of the cue that is fading.

### With QLC+

- **“Save a copy for QLC+”**, in the header of a cuelist, writes another show where the cuelists are steps that QLC+ plays (Sequence or Chaser), with their Cue Lists, keys and controller buttons. The window tells you what QLC+ doesn’t get from each one.
- **Scenes › New › “QLC+ cue lists…”** turns the Cue Lists, chasers and sequences of a QLC+ show into cuelists, with their keys and buttons as mappings.
- A QLC+ chaser can also be converted into a cuelist with its button.

### Palettes

- Palettes **by reference inside steps**: when you update the palette, the steps that use it change.
- Palettes with a **fan**: recorded with a different value on each fixture, the others share it out in the order of the selection.
- **“All”** palettes, with every attribute at once.

### Mappings and controllers

- The **“The selected scene”** target: the last one you pressed in Live, the SHOW, a controller or the phone.
- **“Own colour while fading”** for the LED of a scene (pulsing on the APC mini mk2).
- On the APC40 mkII, the LEDs can pulse or blink.
- The APC mini mk2 goes into Session mode on its own, and “Get ready” in Devices puts any APC back in its mode and sends its LEDs again.
- **Motor faders** no longer fight your hand: while you move one, the program doesn’t move it.

### Super scenes

- Select several blocks (Ctrl+click, Shift+click or a rectangle) and move, delete, duplicate or change them together.
- **Copy, cut and paste** blocks, into another super scene too.
- **Undo and redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y).

### Remote control

- A **QR code** of the address, with the PIN inside: the phone opens the remote without typing anything.
- The phone finds the program by its **name on the network** (`dmxtotalcontrol.local`).
- The SHOW’s **timers**, **DMX levels** and **mixer** now work on the phone.
- Each address has the name of its network in front, with the wifi first.

### FX

- **Shift and delay per fixture**, typed by hand.
- A dimmer FX also works on fixtures without a dimmer (RGB only): it dims all their colours together.
- Macros keep each fixture by its place, and spread over a different number of fixtures.

### More

- Live, a right click on a SHOW button stops what it plays.
- USB DMX interfaces you plug in with the program open show up on their own, and each universe goes back to its interface.
- The manual up to date in all six languages.

Tested with an APC mini mk2 and an FTDI interface (Open DMX). The APC40 mkII, MIDI timecode and phones over wifi still need testing with real gear: if something doesn’t work with yours, [tell us in a bug report](https://github.com/joseramos1999/DMXTotalControl/issues/new/choose).
